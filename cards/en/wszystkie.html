OpenPallet: the standard's cards

OpenPallet 1.2

OpenPallet is an open exchange format for palletizing data: one JSON file describes the pallet, the product, the slip sheets, the layers and every robot move.

An OpenPallet 1.2 file carries the mark "format": {"name": "OpenPallet", "version": "1.2"}, every position in one pallet frame, and every orientation and every direction in degrees counter-clockwise from the top of the drawing.

The same values have a fixed layout for the controller and the robot (PLC structure, CSV), so the PLC and robot program reads what the drawing shows.

Station layout and directions

OpenPallet 1.2

Every element of the station has its own frame and normal pose. The file gives orientations and positions relative to the normal pose: of the conveyor at the pick, of the pallet at the place, however they stand in the station. Pallet frame: X down the drawing along the length (pallet.length), Y to the right along the width (pallet.width), Z up.

Symbol Element
Robot. The integrator sets its frame (Xr, Yr). The robot program places the pallet and the conveyor in it.
Pallet. Origin O in a corner, X along the length (also when the pallet is wider than long), Y along the width, Z up from the deck boards.
Conveyor. Travel down the drawing, the conveyor end (red line) at the bottom.
Gripper at orientation 0. Triangle: the front. Dark bars: sides 90 and 270. Circle: the robot's flange.
Product at orientation 0. Dark bar: face 0 (the front). Blue bar: the label.

  • Orientation (0, 90, 180, 270): the side the front of an element faces. 0 is the normal pose: the product stands as it reaches the conveyor end without being turned, the gripper as it picks most often.
  • The file gives only orientations. How to turn the gripper to reach them safely is the robot program's decision.
  • Direction (compass): the placing direction of the products, in the pallet frame. 900 = neutral: straight down (card 5), no open side and centre (card 4). 999 = not assigned.
  • Move (moves[]): the products the gripper picks and places at once. Z counts from the pallet's deck boards (card 6).

Orientation

OpenPallet 1.2

Orientation is the side the front of an element (face 0 of a product, the front of the gripper) faces in the frame it lies in: 0, 90, 180 or 270, as on the compass. Orientation 0 is the normal pose.

A product in the normal pose (orientation 0) stands as it reaches the conveyor end without being turned: its length along X, face 0 at the top of the drawing.

products at the pick → gripper picks → gripper places → products on the pallet

Step Field in the file Frame example 1 example 2
products at the pick products_at_pick[k].orientation conveyor 270 90
gripper picks gripper_pick_orientation conveyor 0 0
gripper places gripper_place_orientation pallet 90 90
products on the pallet products[].orientation pallet 0 180
  • The product turns together with the gripper. The file gives all four orientations of a move: nothing has to be computed.
  • The controller (PLC) gets the orientations at the pick and both orientations of the gripper. It does not need the orientation on the pallet: the products lie as the gripper placed them.
  • The faces with a label (label_sides of the product type) are given in the normal pose, Product A: [270].

Gripper

OpenPallet 1.2

The gripper at orientation 0 stands with its length along X and its front (the triangle) towards the top of the drawing. At the pick its orientation counts in the conveyor's frame, at the place in the pallet's frame.

We recommend taking the gripper's orientation 0 as the gripper most often picks the products from the conveyor. The file gives the outline of the open gripper, whatever its kind: for a jaw gripper, the outline with the jaws fully open. The outline is usually larger than the products picked. The open side (90 or 270, card 4) must be free when placing: on a jaw gripper it is the side of the moving jaw, on a fork gripper the side the forks slide in from.

  • Gripper orientation at the pick (gripper_pick_orientation): 0 or 180, in the conveyor's frame.
  • Gripper orientation at the place (gripper_place_orientation): 0, 90, 180 or 270, in the pallet frame.
  • Products at the pick (products_at_pick): the products in the order they reach the conveyor end, each with its product number (product_id) and its orientation at the pick (orientation). [0] is the product at the conveyor end, then the next ones from the end.
  • At orientation 0 the back of the gripper lies at the conveyor end, at orientation 180 its front.
  • The allowed gripper orientations at the pick (gripper.allowed_pick_orientation) and at the place (gripper.allowed_place_orientation) are settings of the station.

Open side and alignment

OpenPallet 1.2

The open side (90 or 270) is the side of the gripper that stays free: with the opposite side the gripper sets the products against those already placed. The alignment (0, 180, 900) tells at which end of the gripper the products lie. Both belong to the gripper and count in its frame.

  • Open side (gripper_open_side): 90 or 270. With open side 270 the products lie against side 90, and with that side the gripper sets them against those already placed. Side 270 stays free, unless the station settings say otherwise.
  • Jaw gripper: the side of the moving jaw. Fork gripper: the side the forks come in from. Another kind: the side with the parts that could collide with the neighbouring products.
  • When both sides move, the station allows both (gripper.allowed_open_side).
  • 900: a gripper without an open side (from above). 999: not assigned.
  • Alignment (gripper_align): 0 = products at the gripper's front, 180 = at its back, 900 = in the centre.
  • 900 is not a direction: in the alignment it means the gripper centred on the products.
JSON PLC (tPlacingData, move data) CSV column
open side gripper_open_side: 90, 270, 900 i16GripperOpenSide: 90, 270, 900 GripperOpenSide
alignment gripper_align: 0, 180, 900 i16GripperAlign: 0, 180, 900 GripperAlign

Placing direction

OpenPallet 1.2

The placing direction is the direction in which the gripper with the products travels in the last phase of its approach to the placing position, counted in the pallet frame. 900 means placing straight down from above.

  • 900: straight down from above, without an approach from the side. 999: not assigned.
  • The robot program sets the height and the length of the approach: the file gives only the direction.
JSON PLC (tPlacingData, move data) CSV column
placing direction placing_direction: 0, 45, 90, … 315 i16PlacingDirection: 0, 45, … 315 PlacingDirection
straight down 900 900 900

Stack and slip sheets

OpenPallet 1.2

The stack (stack) lays the layers from the pallet up, each with a slip sheet or without one. Z counts from the pallet's deck boards, so the robot adds the height of the empty pallet (pallet.height) itself.

Z(K) = (K − 1) × product height + thicknesses of the slip sheets under layer K
summary.height = pallet.height + layers + all slip sheets

Another pallet height changes only pallet.height and summary.height. The Z of the layers stays, as it counts from the deck boards.

  • Slip sheet: a sheet on the pallet, between layers or on top.
  • A stack position names a unique layer (layout = id from layouts) and the slip sheet lying on it (slip_sheet = id from slip_sheet_types). No slip_sheet: no slip sheet.
  • A slip sheet type (slip_sheet_types, up to 10 types) has only a thickness (thickness): how much the slip sheet raises the next layer. The integrator sets its size and material.
  • A unique layer (layouts[]): a layer pattern written once. The stack uses it in many layers of the pallet.
  • A product of a move (products[]): x, y = the corner with the smaller X and Y, size_x, size_y = its size along X and Y.
  • The centre of a move for the PLC (tPlacingData) is in whole numbers: trunc cuts off the fraction.

i16MiddleX = trunc((x_min + x_max) / 2)    i16MiddleY = trunc((y_min + y_max) / 2)

OpenPallet 1.2 file

OpenPallet 1.2

An OpenPallet file is JSON. Its top-level keys have a fixed order, and what belongs together lies nested in them: the moves in the unique layer, the products in the move.

  • Recognition: format.name = "OpenPallet" and format.version 1.2 or a later 1.x: read. Another name or version: reject. Skip unknown keys.
  • Later 1.x versions add only optional fields: a program that reads 1.2 reads every later 1.x file.
  • Units: positions and sizes in mm, mass in g, orientations and directions in degrees (whole numbers). Slip sheet thickness and summary.height in mm with one decimal place.
  • A pallet without a mix (summary.mix false): one product type, id 1, and the products in the moves have no field type.
  • Checksum (header.checksum): CRC-32 (8 hex characters) of the JSON without it, keys sorted, no spaces, non-ASCII characters as \uXXXX, whole numbers without .0.
